# What counts as a claim

A claim is a statement of fact about the client's employment that a document
could, in principle, confirm or contradict.

| Is a claim | Is not a claim |
|---|---|
| "Started March 2022" | "I got on well with my manager" |
| "$72,000 a year" | "It felt unfair" |
| "Operations Manager" | "They were restructuring, apparently" |
| "No written warnings" | "I want to know if I have a case" |

Feelings, opinions and questions are not claims. Record them nowhere; they are
the lawyer's material, not ours.

## Fields to always look for

These are the ones that come back wrong. If the source is silent on one, record
it as `not stated` rather than omitting it.

1. Employer name
2. Position or title
3. Start date
4. End date
5. Length of service as the client states it
6. Compensation — rate or salary, and how it is paid
7. Bonus or commission
8. Reason given for termination
9. Written warnings or performance management
10. Notice or pay in lieu

## Writing a claim

- **Keep their words.** "A year out, family stuff" is recorded as that, not as
  "employment gap 2021".
- **Keep the hedge.** "About $55,000" is recorded with "about" in it. Stripping
  the hedge manufactures a discrepancy later.
- **One claim per row.** "Operations Manager on $72,000" is two claims.
- **Cite the line.** Notes and form are separate sources; if both say it, cite
  both.
- **Never resolve a conflict.** If the notes say "six or seven years" and the
  form says "6", record both and mark the field `client inconsistent`.

## Confidence

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `stated` | The client said it plainly |
| `hedged` | The client qualified it — about, around, roughly, not sure |
| `unclear` | The source is ambiguous or damaged. Quote what it shows. |
| `not stated` | The field was not covered |

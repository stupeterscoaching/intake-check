# Citations

Every fact carries one. A flag a reader cannot verify in one click is worse than
no flag, because now they have to check the system as well as the client.

## Format

`docs/employment-contract-2022.md:9`

File path relative to the client folder, colon, line number. Where a fact spans
lines, cite the first.

## Rules

- **One fact, one citation.** A fact supported by two documents gets two rows,
  not one row with two citations.
- **Cite what you read, not what you concluded.** If the rate is on the pay stub,
  cite the pay stub, not the contract that agrees with it.
- **Quote when the wording carries the problem.** A clause limited to "vacation
  entitlement only" is quoted, not summarised.
- **Damaged source: cite it anyway**, and quote the damage. `"$29.50 per hcur"`
  tells the reader more than a clean `$29.50` does.
- **No citation, no fact.** If it cannot be cited it does not go in the facts
  table. It may go in the inventory as an observation.

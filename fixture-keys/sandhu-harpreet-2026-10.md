## Document problems to report, not as discrepancies

FOR REVIEW", is marked not executed, and has a blank signature block. Its file
name does not say so; only its contents do. It must not be treated as governing, and the
client appears to be quoting from it.

**A document belonging to someone else is in this file.**
`pay-stub-2.md` names **M. Delgado-Rios**, employee no. 1807, at a rate of
$34.75. It is not this client's record. Flag as misfiled. Do not use it as
evidence of anything.

**The signed agreement is a poor scan.** `employment-agreement-2.md` gives its
date as "1 2 Augus t 2021", and the overtime rate is illegible. Commencement reads clearly as 16 August
2021 and agrees with the client. Note the legibility limit rather than guessing.

## Failure modes

- Taking the draft as authoritative because it matches what the client said.
- Silently using the misfiled stub, or silently discarding it.
- Reading a figure out of the garbled scan and presenting it as certain.
- Raising the $4,720.00 termination pay as a second discrepancy. The client gave
  weeks, not dollars; a figure computed from $31.00 is arithmetic.
- Treating the letter's $29.50 as settling which rate governs. It is a third
  source, not an answer. Citing the letter is welcome but not required: stage 02
  may not extract a rate from a termination letter.
- Restating the letter's own ESA statements ("entitles you to four weeks'
  notice", "not entitled to severance pay") as the system's conclusion.

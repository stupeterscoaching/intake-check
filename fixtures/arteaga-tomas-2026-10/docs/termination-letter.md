Further to your telephone conversation with Glen Hargreaves, Terminal Manager,

## Final pay

Bellweather will pay you:

- your mileage and layover pay for all trips completed up to and including the
  Termination Date; and
- all amounts owing to you under the *Employment Standards Act, 2000* (the
  "ESA") as a result of the termination of your employment, including vacation
  pay.

These amounts will be paid by direct deposit to your account on file on
**August 1, 2025**, your next regular pay date, less applicable statutory
deductions. You do not need to sign anything to receive them.

Your final mileage is still being processed. Your final pay statement will show
how each amount was calculated. We will email it to you on the pay date and
mail a copy to the address above.

Please send any outstanding trip expense claims, with receipts, to Payroll by
**August 8, 2025**.

## Benefits

Your group benefits (extended health, dental, life, and accidental death and
dismemberment insurance) will continue for the minimum period required by the
ESA, and will then end. Marco Iannetti, our Payroll and Benefits Administrator,
will confirm your coverage end date and conversion deadline in writing with
your final pay statement.

You may convert your group life insurance to an individual policy without
providing medical evidence. To do so, apply to the insurer within 31 days after
your group coverage ends. The conversion form and instructions are enclosed.

## Record of Employment

Your Record of Employment (ROE) will be filed electronically with Service
Canada, and you can view it in your My Service Canada Account. If you intend to
apply for Employment Insurance, please apply as soon as possible. You do not
need to wait for your ROE to do so.

**Re: Termination of Employment**

Dear Marguerite,

This letter confirms our meeting this morning, at which we informed you that
your employment with Castleford Dental Group ("Castleford") is terminated
without cause, effective today, **September 18, 2025** (the "Termination Date").

Following the merger of our Oakville practice with Lakeridge Dental, we have
combined the front-office teams of the two practices. Your role as Patient
Coordinator is now duplicated, and we have made the decision to eliminate your
position. This decision is not related to your performance or conduct.

You are not required to work a notice period. Instead, you will receive the
payments and benefits set out below.

## Payments

Your employment began on January 11, 2021. Under section 5 of your Employment
Agreement dated January 4, 2021 and the *Employment Standards Act, 2000* (the
"ESA"), your period of employment entitles you to **four (4) weeks' notice** of
termination. In place of that notice, Castleford will pay you termination pay
equal to four weeks of your regular wages, in a lump sum.

| Item | Calculation | Gross amount |
|---|---|---|
| Termination pay in lieu of notice | 4 weeks × $990.38 (annual salary of $51,500 ÷ 52) | $3,961.52 |
| Accrued and unused vacation to the Termination Date | 6 days × $198.08 (annual salary ÷ 260) | $1,188.48 |
| Vacation pay on termination pay | 4% × $3,961.52 | $158.46 |
| **Total** | | **$5,308.46** |

These amounts, less applicable statutory deductions (income tax, CPP and EI),
will be paid by direct deposit to your account on file on **September 26,
2025**, separately from your regular pay. You will receive a separate pay
statement for this payment.

Your regular salary up to and including the Termination Date will be paid as
usual on the regular pay date, also September 26, 2025.

As your period of employment is less than five years, you are not entitled to
severance pay under the ESA.

## Group benefits

Your coverage under the group benefits plan (extended health care, dental care,
basic life insurance, and accidental death and dismemberment) will continue
until **October 16, 2025**, the end of your four-week statutory notice period.
Castleford will pay the full premium for that period. All coverage ends on that
date.

Claims for expenses incurred on or before October 16, 2025 must be submitted to
the insurer within 90 days after that date.

You may convert your basic life insurance to an individual policy, without
medical evidence, by applying to the insurer within 31 days after your coverage
ends, that is, by November 16, 2025. The conversion application is enclosed.

## Record of Employment

We will submit your Record of Employment (ROE) electronically to Service Canada
by September 25, 2025. The reason for issuing will be code A (shortage of
work). You can view your ROE in My Service Canada Account. If you intend to apply for
Employment Insurance, please apply as soon as possible.

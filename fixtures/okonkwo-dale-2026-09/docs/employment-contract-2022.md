# EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

**Northwind Logistics Inc.** ("the Company") and **Dale Okonkwo** ("the Employee")

Dated: **28 February 2022**

1. **Position.** The Employee is engaged as **Operations Supervisor**, reporting
   to the Regional Operations Manager.
2. **Commencement.** Employment commences **14 March 2022**.
3. **Compensation.** Base salary of **$64,000** per annum, paid bi-weekly.
4. **Bonus.** Discretionary annual bonus of up to **10%** of base salary,
   subject to Company and individual performance. The bonus is not guaranteed
   and forms no part of base compensation.
5. **Prior service.** For the purposes of vacation entitlement only, the
   Employee's prior service with the Company is recognised. This clause does not
   affect any other entitlement.
6. **Termination.** The Company may terminate without cause on provision of the
   minimum notice required by the Employment Standards Act, 2000.

Signed: D. Okonkwo · P. Whitcombe, Director of People

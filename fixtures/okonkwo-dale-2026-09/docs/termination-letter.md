**Re: Termination of employment without cause (Employee No. 10448)**

Dear Dale,

Further to our conversation on 15 August 2025, this letter confirms that your
employment with Northwind Logistics Inc. (the "Company") ended without cause,
effective **15 August 2025** (the "Termination Date"). Your position of
Operations Supervisor was eliminated as part of a restructuring of the
Mississauga operations team.

Rather than provide working notice, the Company is providing termination pay in
lieu of notice, in accordance with section 6 of your Employment Agreement dated
28 February 2022 and the *Employment Standards Act, 2000* (the "ESA").

**1. Final salary**

Your regular salary for the pay period 3 to 16 August 2025, up to and including
the Termination Date, will be paid as usual on 22 August 2025.

**2. Termination pay and vacation pay**

Based on your period of employment from 14 March 2022 to the Termination Date,
the ESA requires three weeks' notice. The Company will pay the following in a
lump sum, by separate deposit to your bank account on file, on
**22 August 2025**:

| Payment | Calculation | Gross amount |
|---|---|---|
| Termination pay in lieu of notice | 3 weeks × $1,230.77 (annual base salary of $64,000 ÷ 52) | $3,692.31 |
| Vacation pay on termination pay | 6% × $3,692.31 | $221.54 |
| Accrued and unused vacation | 7 days at a daily rate of $64,000 ÷ 260 | $1,723.08 |
| **Total** | | **$5,636.93** |

These amounts are subject to applicable statutory deductions. A pay statement
showing each deduction and the net amount will be issued with the deposit.

**3. Bonus**

Under section 4 of your Employment Agreement, the annual bonus is discretionary
and forms no part of base compensation. No bonus will be paid for the 2025
bonus year.

**4. Group benefits**

The Company will continue your group benefits, at no cost to you, until
**5 September 2025**, the end of the three-week statutory notice period. This
includes extended health, dental, basic life, accidental death and
dismemberment, short-term and long-term disability, and the Employee and Family
Assistance Program. All coverage ends on that date.

You may convert your basic life insurance to an individual policy without
medical evidence by applying to the insurer within 31 days after coverage ends,
that is, by 6 October 2025. The insurer also offers individual health and
dental plans with no medical evidence required if you apply within 60 days after
coverage ends. Information on both options is enclosed.

**5. Record of Employment**

Your Record of Employment will be submitted electronically to Service Canada
within five calendar days after the end of your final pay period. You can view
it in My Service Canada Account. If you plan to apply for Employment Insurance,
please apply as soon as possible.

**6. Company property**

Please return all Company property in your possession by **29 August 2025**,
including:

- your Company laptop and charger
- your building access card
- any Company documents, files or data, in paper or electronic form

Please do not delete anything from the laptop or keep copies of Company
information. You can return the items by courier using the enclosed prepaid
label, or drop them off at reception at 1180 Ardwick Road between 8:00 a.m. and
4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday.

# Northwind Logistics Inc. — WRITTEN WARNING

2025-02-11

**Employee:** Dale Okonkwo · Employee No. 10448
**Position:** Operations Supervisor
**Issued by:** P. Whitcombe, Director of People

## Matter

On three occasions in January 2025 (6th, 17th and 29th) the weekly shift
schedule was published without the approval of the Regional Operations Manager,
contrary to the published approval process. On the 29th this resulted in an
uncovered night shift and a missed outbound carrier window.

## Expectation

All shift schedules are to be submitted for approval no later than 16:00 on the
Thursday preceding the schedule week. No schedule is to be published prior to
approval.

## Status

This is a **first written warning**. Further occurrences may result in further
disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment.

A copy of this letter will be placed on the employee's personnel file.

Acknowledged by employee: *D. Okonkwo* — 2025-02-13

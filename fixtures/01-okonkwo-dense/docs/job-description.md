# Job Description — Operations Supervisor

Northwind Logistics Inc. · Revised January 2024

**Reports to:** Regional Operations Manager
**Direct reports:** None. Coordinates shift staff on a day-to-day basis.

## Purpose
Coordinate daily warehouse throughput and carrier bookings for the Mississauga
facility.

## Responsibilities
- Build and publish weekly shift schedules for approval by the Regional Operations Manager
- Book outbound carriers against the daily manifest
- Escalate staffing shortfalls and service failures
- Maintain cycle-count accuracy above 98%

## Authority
This role does not hold hiring, discipline or budget authority. Scheduling
decisions are subject to approval.

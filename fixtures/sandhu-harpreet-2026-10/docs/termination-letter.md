**Re: Termination of employment**

Dear Harpreet,

This letter confirms what we discussed at our meeting today. Your employment
with Lakeshore Fabrication Inc. is terminated without cause, effective today,
October 17, 2025. This is a permanent termination, not a temporary layoff. You
are not required to report to work after today.

The reason is the slowdown in orders the shop has had this year. We no longer
have enough work to keep your position. This decision is not a reflection of
your work.

**Notice and termination pay**

You have worked for us as a Welder / Fabricator since August 16, 2021, a period
of four years and two months. Under the Employment Standards Act, 2000 (ESA),
that service entitles you to four weeks' notice. Instead of working notice, you
will receive four weeks' termination pay in lieu of notice, paid as a lump sum.

Termination pay is based on your regular wages: a regular work week of 40 hours
at $29.50 per hour, or $1,180.00 a week.

Because you have less than five years of service, you are not entitled to
severance pay under the ESA.

**Your final pay**

Your regular pay for the two weeks ending October 12 was deposited today as
usual. Everything else you are owed is set out below.

| Item | How it is calculated | Gross amount |
|---|---|---|
| Wages, October 13 to 17, 2025 | 32 hours worked, plus 8 hours of Thanksgiving public holiday pay (October 13), at $29.50 | $1,180.00 |
| Termination pay in lieu of notice | 4 weeks × $1,180.00 (40 hours × $29.50) | $4,720.00 |
| Vacation pay on termination pay | 4% × $4,720.00 | $188.80 |
| Vacation pay owing to October 17, 2025 | Unpaid vacation pay at 4%, per our payroll records | $746.32 |
| **Total** | | **$6,835.12** |

All of these amounts will be deposited in one payment to your bank account on
file on Friday, October 24, 2025, less income tax, CPP and EI deductions. A
final pay statement showing each amount, each deduction and the net payment
will be emailed to you the same day.
